Key Features

Designed for Modern Healthcare

DocConnect streamlines hospital workflows, eliminating long waiting lines and lost paper prescriptions.

30-Min Intelligent Slots

Appointments are booked in 30-minute intervals with a strict cap of 3 patients per slot to eliminate waiting room crowding.

Smart Token Generation

Patients receive automated sequential tokens (Token #1, #2, #3) per slot, making it effortless to know their exact turn.

Timed Dosage & Blur Stamp

Prescriptions enforce an active dosage timeline. Once expired, prescriptions are safeguarded with a clear dosage expiry watermark.

Admin-Verified Doctors

Doctors register under pending review. Only after admin verification is authorization granted, ensuring 100% genuine medical care.

Role-Segregated Privacy

Patients access only their own medical history and slips. Doctors securely author and view prescriptions for their assigned consultations.

Downloadable Slips

Download professional appointment and prescription slips directly to your device with doctor credentials, tokens, and dates.
